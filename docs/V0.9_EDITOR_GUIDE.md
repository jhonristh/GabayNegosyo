# GabayNegosyo v0.9: scope and content editing

## Scope
The platform organizes requirements from BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF), and the city or municipality (LGU) responsible for business permits. Registration or renewal through agencies outside these five is not presented as an in-scope checklist item. Historical client PDF references remain archived in `docs/client-sources/`; their mentions of other agencies are not active website content.

## Admin workflow
Open Admin, review the count of content requiring attention, then select Agencies, Requirements, Resources, or Tutorials. Agency records are fixed to five. Requirements support form-based editing of description, audience, preparation list, steps, and sources. Resources use a title, category, agency, URL, description, and checked date. Tutorials accept a YouTube URL and can remain marked “awaiting verification.” Preview links are offered before saving. The Users page is read-only and comes from Supabase.

**Current persistence limit:** Content edits still use the existing browser-local override store. They are visible only in that browser and will disappear if its data is cleared. They are not published to other visitors or synchronized through Supabase. A shared live admin CMS requires a planned Supabase content migration and server-side authorization; this release does not claim to provide that.

**Compliance limit:** Newly entered requirements are marked as placeholders. The form preserves existing applicability and date fields when editing but does not configure new rule-engine matching. Do not treat a newly added item as an approved personalized obligation until its conditions and date behavior are reviewed.

## Media previews
A public YouTube video can play in an iframe using its video ID; no Google Data API key is needed for ordinary embedding. The owner can disallow embeds. Current form previews cover direct PDFs served from the allowed BIR CDN; the page always offers a source link when embedding fails. Other document hosts need explicit validation and an appropriate Content Security Policy before inline preview; uploads and file hosting are not part of this version.

## Credits
The landing page credits the fourth-year BS Management Accounting research project and displays four generic portrait/name slots. Confirm the number of researchers and their actual names, roles, school, photos, permission to publish, and website contributors before replacing placeholders.
