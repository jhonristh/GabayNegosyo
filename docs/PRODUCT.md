# Product requirements

## Purpose

Help Philippine microbusiness owners identify relevant compliance items, understand the next step, and track completion. The product is independent informational guidance and does not replace an agency or professional adviser.

## Audience and core journey

Online sellers, freelancers and small business owners answer the registration wizard (no account needed), receive an applicable checklist, review agency and deadline details, and monitor progress on the dashboard after signing up.

## Scope

- Agencies: BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund and the LGU responsible for business permits. Registration or renewal through other agencies is not presented as an in-scope checklist item. DTI business-name and BMBE material appears as resources and guide content only.
- Coverage: Metro Manila (NCR). National rules are independent of locality; local permit guidance is general, not verified per LGU.
- Public: landing page, legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Disclaimer), Credits, guest wizard and preview.
- Signed in: checklist, requirement detail, deadlines, registration and renewal guides, BIR forms, penalties, resources, tutorials, account and Premium views.
- Admin: content management routes and read-only user views.

## Plan boundaries

Free shows what a requirement is, who needs it, why it is on your checklist, its official source and content status. Premium adds documents, steps, forms, tutorials, the penalty simulator and email reminders. The Premium upgrade is a demo and is not a payment checkout. Never present outline content as verified.

## Content integrity

- Every requirement carries a `contentSource` (`gabay_guide`, `gabay_wizard`, `prototype_placeholder`) so placeholder content is never mistaken for sourced content.
- Deadlines are only shown as dates where the source gives a fixed date; otherwise the app says "No fixed date" and shows the timing rule.
- The landing preview is illustrative and labelled so.
- Penalty estimates and deadlines must be verified against official sources before real reliance. Do not imply automatic official filing or complete nationwide coverage.
- No invented requirements, contact details, names, videos or links.

## Design acceptance

At wide desktop widths the landing hero and dashboard use the available width; landing CTAs open the wizard or sign-up; section navigation reaches real sections; the dashboard foregrounds progress and the next pending item. At narrow mobile widths content stacks, nothing scrolls sideways, keyboard focus stays visible, reduced motion is respected and zoom is never blocked.

## Future evaluation (proposed, not observed)

First-time wizard completion, checklist opening, return visits to deadlines, mobile navigation usability and verified content coverage.
