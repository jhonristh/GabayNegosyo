# GabayNegosyo — Product Requirements (v0.5)

## Purpose
Help Philippine microbusiness owners identify relevant compliance items, understand the next step, and track completion. The product is independent informational guidance and does not replace an agency or professional adviser.

## Audience and core journey
Online sellers, freelancers, and small business owners create an account, answer the registration wizard, receive an applicable checklist, review agency and deadline details, and monitor progress on the dashboard.

## Current scope
- Public landing with clear invitation into signup and an illustrative product preview.
- Authenticated wizard, checklist, requirement detail, deadlines, resources, tutorials, account and premium views.
- Admin management routes and existing Supabase authentication and user data flow.
- Responsive desktop and mobile navigation, functional CTA links, legal pages and PWA shell.

## Plan boundaries
Free checklist displays requirement name, agency and deadline. Premium surfaces additional guidance, documents, tutorials and the penalty estimator where data is available. The existing demo upgrade is not a payment checkout. Never label outline content as verified.

## v0.5 design acceptance
At a wide desktop viewport, the landing hero and dashboard use the available width; the landing CTA opens signup; section navigation reaches actual sections; the dashboard foregrounds progress and its next pending item. At narrow mobile widths, content stacks and remains navigable. Focus indicators and reduced motion remain supported. No changes to Supabase credentials, schema, queries, authentication, eligibility rules or requirement dataset are included in this design release.

## Content integrity and limitations
The landing preview is illustrative. BIR sourced content and other agencies' outline content are described distinctly. Deadlines and penalty estimates must be verified against official sources before real reliance. Existing data and flows remain authoritative; do not imply automatic official filing or complete nationwide coverage.

## Future evaluation
Check first-time wizard completion, checklist opening, return visits to deadlines, mobile navigation usability and verified content coverage. These are proposed measures; the project does not claim observed performance.
